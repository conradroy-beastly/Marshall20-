LeapEd / from news to action

LEAPED / ACTIONABLE EDUCATION

Johor’s phased reopening: build a stronger learning-continuity system

Johor’s updated plan limits reopening on 26–27 October to examination candidates, with all schools scheduled to resume on 28 October subject to air quality. This supersedes the earlier expectation of a general Monday return. For LeapEd, the actionable development is the need to protect learning through repeated closures and uneven attendance while following official health and safety decisions.

Evidence limitations: This is an operational policy report, not an evaluation of learning loss or a proven intervention. It supplies no pupil-level outcomes. Reopening depends on conditions, and a short pilot cannot establish causal impact without a suitable comparison.

Proposed pilot: partner with four willing schools for six weeks. Adapt READy+ into short paper and phone-accessible literacy/numeracy activities; offer weekly teacher coaching and a common family communication routine. On safe return, use brief diagnostics to target small-group catch-up. Obtain school approval, protect pupil data and record safeguarding concerns through existing school procedures.

Proposed success measures: contact at least 90% of families within 48 hours of a closure; weekly participation of at least 80%; completion of at least 85% of planned coaching sessions; paired baseline/endline reading and number-skill checks, with results split by starting level and access needs. Compare progress with similar non-pilot classes where feasible, track teacher workload, and scale only if learning and access improve without excessive burden.
