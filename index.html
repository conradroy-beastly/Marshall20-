Small AI, practical classroom support

The World Bank reports pilots including phone-based teacher coaching in Ethiopia, career guidance in Peru and a planned maths tutor within Ukraine’s existing education platform. The emphasis is on specific classroom needs and testing before scaling.

Why LeapEd should care: This connects directly to READy+ learning recovery, teacher capacity building and the Cycle of Inquiry. Published three days ago, it is a strategic development rather than breaking news today.

Evidence limit: Early pilots and reported experiences do not establish impact in Malaysian schools. Ukraine’s tutor is planned; its intended reach is not achieved reach.

A practical LeapEd pilot

Our proposed adaptation, not a recommendation made by the source.

01 / Teachers first

Test for eight weeks with 10 READy+ teachers in two or three schools. Use a teacher-facing assistant to draft differentiated BM/English activities and numeracy prompts from approved materials.

02 / Coaching and safeguards

Integrate review into existing PLCs. Teachers check all outputs, and a LeapEd coach samples accuracy weekly. Begin without identifiable pupil data or classroom recordings.

03 / Measure before expanding

Track preparation time, material quality, teacher uptake and pupil progress using comparable baseline/endline tasks completed without AI. Include a comparison group where feasible. Agree success thresholds and full cost per teacher in advance.

Sponsor proposition: Fund a measured trial of stronger teaching and learning, with a review of results before expansion.
